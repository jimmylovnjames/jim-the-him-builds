# Jim the Him

**[Play in your browser](https://jimmylovnjames.github.io/jim-the-him-builds/)** (phone or desktop, no install). Hold your phone sideways.

## Android

**[Download Jim the Him for Android](https://jimmylovnjames.github.io/jim-the-him-builds/jim-the-him.apk)** (v1.4.0, 86.6 MB). This link always gets the latest version.

- Android 7.0 or newer, 64-bit and 32-bit ARM phones.
- Eight stages, from Waimamaku Bush to the Graft Corp Fortress, with touch controls.
- New in 1.4.0: the shed's upgrades come nine to a page with big PREV / NEXT buttons, so every BUY button is

  Smokey's head torch glows (and lights the way on the night stage).
- New in 1.3.0: fighters level up (to 30); gold-lit champions; 12 taonga to find and wear (three at a time);
  three stars per stage; Smokey Baboom is playable; Scotch with short hair and no beard; the Uncs with darker skin;
  Jim clean-shaven with his machete, staff and sledgehammer; livelier sprites.
- New in 1.2.1: the shed's upgrade list scrolls with a finger swipe, so every upgrade (Double Jump included) can be bought on a phone.
- New in 1.2.0: Jim fights with a machete, a long staff, a big chain and revolvers; a different transformation
  every stage; and a crew who join as you clear stages (Hillman Hunter, Scotch Screwdriver, The Uncs).

## Install

1. **Updating to v1.4.0? Uninstall Jim the Him first.** The signing key used for v1.2.0 to v1.3.1 was lost, so
   v1.4.0 is signed with a new one and Android says "App not installed" over an older build. Uninstalling clears
   the old save; this happens once. From v1.4.0 on, every update installs over the top and keeps your save, and
   **BACKUP** in the shed lets you keep a copy of your progress as a code.
2. Download the APK on the phone and open it from the download notification or the Files app, then tap **Install**.
   If Android asks, allow **Install unknown apps** for that app.
3. If Play Protect warns about an unknown developer, tap **More details**, then **Install anyway**.
