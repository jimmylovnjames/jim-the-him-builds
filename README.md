# Jim the Him

**[Play in your browser](https://jimmylovnjames.github.io/jim-the-him-builds/)** (phone or desktop, no install). Hold your phone sideways.

## Android

**[Download jim-the-him-v1.2.0.apk](https://jimmylovnjames.github.io/jim-the-him-builds/jim-the-him-v1.2.0.apk)** (84.5 MB)

- Android 7.0 or newer, 64-bit and 32-bit ARM phones.
- Eight stages, from Waimamaku Bush to the Graft Corp Fortress, with touch controls.
- New in 1.2.0: Jim fights with a machete, a long staff, a big chain and revolvers; a different transformation
  every stage; and a crew who join as you clear stages (Hillman Hunter, Scotch Screwdriver, The Uncs).

## Install

1. **Coming from v1.1.3 or older?** Uninstall it first. 1.2.0 is signed with a new key, so Android says
   "App not installed" otherwise. Uninstalling clears the old save; this happens once, and every update after
   1.2.0 installs over the top and keeps your save.
2. Download the APK on the phone and open it from the download notification or the Files app, then tap **Install**.
   If Android asks, allow **Install unknown apps** for that app.
3. If Play Protect warns about an unknown developer, tap **More details**, then **Install anyway**.
